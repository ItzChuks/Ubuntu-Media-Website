00:01:00 — Who We Are

An independent screen production and media company.

Ubuntu Media is based in Halifax, Nova Scotia, working across documentary and narrative film, podcasting, artist management, photography, and digital media. Our name comes from the South African idea of Ubuntu, I am because you are, and it holds everything we do together.
