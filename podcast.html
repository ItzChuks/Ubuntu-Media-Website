Why We Podcast

Some stories need a mic, not a lens.

Film and installation carry the visual work. The podcasts carry what happens when the cameras are off and people just talk. Both shows grew out of the same instinct as the rest of the slate, find someone worth listening to, then get out of the way. If you're building a show of your own, this is also where our podcast production service lives, planning, recording, editing, branding, and network development, learned first-hand on our own feeds.
