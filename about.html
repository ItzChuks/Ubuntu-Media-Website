Let's Tell Your Story
Who We Are

Story is the throughline.

An independent screen production and media company based in Halifax, Nova Scotia, working across documentary and narrative film, podcasting, artist management, photography, and digital media.

I am because you are.

Our name comes from the South African idea of Ubuntu, I am because you are, and it holds everything we do together. It is a philosophy before it is a company name: none of us tells a story alone, and no story worth telling belongs to only one person.

We believe a good story changes how people see each other. So we tell stories that carry Afrocentric and immigrant experience to broadcast, festival, and gallery audiences, and we help brands and organisations tell theirs. Whether the screen is in a cinema, a phone, or a museum wall, the work starts the same way, with someone worth listening to.

That is why the studio sits across so many forms at once. A documentary and a corporate video are not different disciplines to us, they are the same instinct pointed at different rooms: find the person worth listening to, and get out of their way.

Story is the throughline. It is not a format we work in, it is the reason we work.
What We Hold To

Three things that don't change.

Someone worth listening to

Every project starts with a person, not a pitch. If there isn't someone worth listening to at the centre of it, we haven't found the story yet.

Craft over trend

We would rather take longer and get the cut, the light, or the mix right than ship something fast that doesn't hold up on a second watch.

Rooted, not niche

Halifax is home base, but the stories we carry, Afrocentric and immigrant experience among them, travel to broadcast, festival, and gallery audiences well beyond it.

How We Work

From conversation to premiere.

The same four moves, whether it's a feature documentary or a thirty-second brand film.

01

Listen

Every project begins as a conversation, not a brief. We spend real time with the people and the subject before a camera comes out.

02

Develop

Treatment, structure, and funding strategy, shaped alongside broadcasters, festivals, and development programs where the project calls for it.

03

Produce

Production and post, run with an eco-conscious approach that plans shoots to reduce waste and carbon footprint from the outset.

04

Share

Broadcast, festival, gallery wall, or podcast feed, whichever platform the story actually needs to be seen and heard on.
