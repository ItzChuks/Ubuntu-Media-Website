The Work

The slate.

Documentary, narrative feature, series, and installation, in production, in development, and on stage. This is the proof.

Wrestling Saved My Life

Wrestling Saved My Life

A documentary about the ring as a place to survive and be seen.

Inside gyms and under the lights, the film follows the wrestlers for whom the ring was never just a sport, it was the one place they were allowed to be fully seen.

Documentary — In Post-Production
Between Lands

Between Lands

A site-specific installation on migration and belonging, at the Canadian Museum of Immigration at Pier 21.

Staged at the very pier where so many arrivals to Canada began, the installation sits visitors inside the space between where you're from and where you've landed.

Nocturne 2026 — October 17
The Space Between

The Space Between

A narrative feature in development on the distance between who we were and who we are becoming.

Developed through the Berlinale EFM Fiction Toolbox and Telefilm Canada's Development Program, the script is being shaped alongside international story editors.

Berlinale EFM Fiction Toolbox — Telefilm Development Program
Run

Run

A narrative feature in development, drawing on the life and conviction of Nelson Mandela.

Early-stage development on a feature that draws its conviction, rather than its plot beat for beat, from Mandela's own life.

In Development
East Out

East Out

A documentary series following the pull between home and away.

Across several episodes, East Out sits with people making, or unmaking, the decision to leave home behind.

Aired on Bell Fibe TV
Afraid of the Dark / The Spa

Afraid of the Dark & The Spa

Two narrative features in development, extending the slate into genre and tension.

A pair of features that push the studio's storytelling instincts into sharper, more suspenseful territory, still built around people worth watching closely.

In Development
